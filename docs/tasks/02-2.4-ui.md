## Aniq vazifa (har biri alohida faylda, `index.ts` orqali eksport, accessibility, testlar)
1. **`Table`** (+ `TableHeader`, `TableRow`, `TableHead`, `TableCell`, `TableBody`, `TableEmpty`): oddiy chegaralar, sarlavhada ixtiyoriy saralash tugmasi (`aria-sort`, `onSort`), mobil ekranda gorizontal scroll konteyner, `TableEmpty` (EmptyState bilan).
2. **`Pagination`**: `page`, `pageSize`, `total`, `onPageChange`; oldingi/keyingi + sahifa raqamlari (ko'p bo'lsa qisqartirilgan), `aria-current`, "N ta natija" matni.
3. **`Select`**: tabiiy `<select>` asosida (accessibility va sodda wireframe uchun), `FormField` bilan mos (`id`, `aria-invalid`, `aria-describedby`), `placeholder` option.
4. **`SearchInput`**: Input + qidiruv belgisi o'rniga oddiy label/placeholder, tozalash tugmasi; debounce komponentda emas (feature hook'ida — hisobotda yoz).
5. **`Dialog`**: modal oyna — `@radix-ui/react-dialog` (aniq pin, ≥2 hafta oldin chiqqan stable; accessibility: fokus tuzog'i, Esc, `aria-labelledby`) yoki o'z yechim — asosla. `Dialog`, `DialogTrigger`, `DialogContent`, `DialogTitle`, `DialogDescription`, `DialogFooter`. Fon qoplamasi oddiy (blur yo'q, mavjud token'lardan; shaffoflik uchun token kerak bo'lsa — Tailwind `opacity`/`bg-text/50` kabi semantik token + opacity modifikatori).
6. **`ConfirmDialog`**: tasdiqlash (masalan deaktivatsiya, tokenni bekor qilish) — `title`, `description`, `confirmLabel`, `variant` (`danger`), `loading`, `onConfirm`.
7. **`Badge`**: `variant` — `neutral`, `success`, `warning`, `danger`, `info` (status: ACTIVE/INACTIVE, ARCHIVED va h.k.).
8. **`Tabs`**: `@radix-ui/react-tabs` yoki o'z yechim (roving tabindex, `role=tablist/tab/tabpanel`) — asosla.
9. **`Textarea`**: Input bilan bir xil uslub va xato holati.
10. **`FileInput`**: CSV tanlash — label, tanlangan fayl nomi, `accept`, xato holati (drag-drop shart emas).
11. **`SecretReveal`**: vaqtinchalik parol yoki ota-ona tokeni/havolasini BIR MARTA ko'rsatish — monospace qiymat, "Nusxa olish" tugmasi (`navigator.clipboard`, muvaffaqiyat holati `aria-live`), ogohlantirish matni ("Bu qiymat faqat hozir ko'rsatiladi") — `Alert` warning bilan.
12. **`DescriptionList`** (`dl`): profil/tafsilot sahifalari uchun "nom — qiymat" juftlari.
13. `/design-preview` ga barcha yangi komponentlar va holatlari.
